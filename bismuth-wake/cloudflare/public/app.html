Pick a version and loader, then press start server. Changing version or loader re-downloads the jar.

fabric — optimized auto-installs Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix, Krypton, Starlight/ScalableLux and C2ME, and locks them on (mods tab won't let you remove them). Together they're the standard vanilla-preserving stack for trimming RAM/CPU on small servers, tuned here with view-distance 8 / simulation-distance 6 for ~4 players. C2ME specifically parallelizes chunk generation/loading across CPU cores for faster exploration — its one known quirk is that regenerating the same seed can produce very slightly different terrain run-to-run, since generation order is no longer single-threaded; no gameplay mechanic changes, just how the terrain gets computed. paper/vanilla don't support these mods (different mod format), so pick fabric if you want them. Very old versions (pre-1.14) don't have Fabric builds — those fall back to Paper automatically.
